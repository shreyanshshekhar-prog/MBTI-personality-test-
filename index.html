The 4 Core Cognitive Dimensions

Energy: Extraversion (E) vs Introversion (I)

Where you draw and replenish your mental stamina. Extraverts thrive on outer action and social momentum; Introverts recharge in solitary reflection and focused depth.

Information: Sensing (S) vs Intuition (N)

How you process information. Sensing types focus on concrete, present facts and practical experience; Intuitive types read between the lines, focusing on patterns and future possibilities.

Decisions: Thinking (T) vs Feeling (F)

How you weigh dilemmas and make conclusions. Thinkers seek objective logic, rational principles, and fairness; Feelers prioritize empathy, human relationships, and emotional harmony.

Lifestyle: Judging (J) vs Perceiving (P)

How you structure your outer world. Judging types favor checklists, definite plans, and decisive closure; Perceiving types prefer flexible spontaneity, improvisation, and open options.
